# Weapon distinctness

Each weapon's showcase scenario rendered through the whole audio system with engines, interface and music silent, cut to three seconds from its first sound, and reduced to a fingerprint: spectral centroid and spread (octaves), flatness (0 tone, 1 noise), audible length, the loudness shape in eight slices, the strongest frequency in each quarter, and where the loudest slice falls. The distance scales each of those so that 1 is plainly different on that axis alone. The test fails under 0.9.

**Closest pair: `weapon_tesla` and `weapon_saw`, distance 1.71.** This is a measurement of difference in the signal, not a judgement by ear; no person has listened.
